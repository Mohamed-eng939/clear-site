How we work

Four steps, in writing, with a review at every stage.

Step 1

A short call

Your learners, your levels, your textbooks and what is not working today.

Step 2

A written scope

What we will produce, how many levels, the timeline and the price. Usage terms are agreed in writing before work starts.

Step 3

Staged delivery

We deliver level by level. You review each stage before we move on, so nothing arrives as a surprise.

Step 4

Handover

Editable files in your branding, and a short walkthrough so your teachers know how to use them.

What we build

Choose one service or a full program.

Level division and course map

Your program split into CEFR-aligned levels, with lesson counts, can-do goals and a clear path from first lesson to last.

Lesson plans and teacher guides

Staged plans with timings, teacher notes, answer keys and homework, so any trained teacher delivers the same standard.

Placement tests and level reports

Test design, a scoring guide and a report format that tells learners their level, what they can do and what to build next.

Teacher development

Observation checklists, feedback templates and short training sessions for your teaching team.

Questions

Do you copy from textbooks?

No. We can map your program to the topics and levels of the book you already use, but we write the lessons, activities and wording ourselves.

What happens to our materials and data?

They stay confidential. We never reuse one client’s material for another, and we never show a client’s work in our portfolio without their written permission. Our sample templates use placeholder content only.

How much does it cost?

We quote after a short call, because the scope differs: one level, a full program or a placement test. The written scope states the price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. The written scope gives a timeline, and we deliver level by level so you can start using finished levels while we build the next. A sample handout takes 2 working days.

Do you work with independent trainers?

Yes. If you teach privately, we can build your level map, lesson plans or placement test in the same way.
