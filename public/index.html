Questions

Are lessons one-to-one?

Yes. Every lesson is just you and your teacher, online, for one hour. There are no groups. We send you the video-call link before your first lesson, and all you need is a phone, tablet or laptop with a steady internet connection.

How long is a level?

A level is eight one-hour lessons. Most learners have one or two lessons a week, so a level takes about four to eight weeks. A level is a block of study, not a whole CEFR band: moving from A1 to A2, for example, can take more than one block, and your report tells you honestly how many to expect.

What happens in the placement test, and is it included?

Yes, it is included in the price of every level. It is a speaking test of about 15 minutes with a teacher on video. You do not need to prepare and there is nothing to pass or fail. Within two working days you receive your written level report (your CEFR level, what you can already do and what to build next) and your personal plan for the eight lessons.

I am a beginner, or nervous about speaking. Is that fine?

Yes. A1 is a normal starting point, and many learners come back to English after years away. The test begins with very simple questions, your teacher speaks slowly, and you can ask for anything to be repeated. Nobody hears you except your teacher.

Which program should I choose?

General English is for everyday life: travel, family, school meetings and building confidence. Business English is for the workplace: meetings, emails, presentations and calls. Conversation is for fluency, with live speaking and detailed feedback on your accuracy and vocabulary. ESP is English for your own field, such as media, politics, accounting or another profession. Not sure? Book the placement test and your teacher will recommend one. You can switch program between levels.

Can I study from another country?

Yes. Learners join from Egypt and from abroad. Tell us your city when you book and we will offer lesson times in your local time.

What materials do I get?

Course materials for your level, shared digitally before each lesson, and self-study tasks to do between lessons. Your teacher reviews the self-study work at the start of the next lesson, so nothing you practise is wasted.

How do I book, and how do I pay?

Choose a package, then book your placement test as your first session. You pay for your package to confirm it. Prices are in US dollars. We send you the payment link on WhatsApp, and a receipt once it is paid. The receipt names the program and package you chose, and we keep a copy too. Packages of two or three levels are cheaper per level than a single level.

How do I reschedule a lesson?

Message the academy team on WhatsApp, not your teacher directly, and we will arrange a new time with both of you. Please give us at least 24 hours' notice. Later than that, the lesson counts as taken. If we have to cancel, we reschedule it. The eight lessons of a level are valid for ten weeks, so you can fit them around work and family. If you need a break, you can pause for up to one month and we will hold your lesson slot. After a month we release the slot and offer you the next available time. The pause counts within the ten weeks.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are not refundable, because your payment reserves your teacher’s time for the whole level. If you cannot continue, you can transfer your unused lessons to another learner you refer, and we will set them up on the same package. If the match is not right, we can change your teacher or your program. If we cannot deliver lessons you have paid for, we refund them or reschedule them. If the law where you live gives you a right to cancel an online purchase, you keep it: write to us within 14 days of paying, and we refund the lessons you have not used, minus payment fees. The details are in the refund policy below.
